Wen-Shuai Hu (胡文帅)

Assistant Professor at Southwest Jiaotong University (SWJTU)

I am currently a Lecturer with the School of Information Science and Technology, Southwest Jiaotong University, Chengdu, China. I received my Ph.D. degree in 2022 from the School of Information Science and Technology, Southwest Jiaotong University, under the supervision of Prof. Heng-Chao Li.

My current interests include intelligent interpretation and applications of remote sensing images, computer vision and pattern recognition, deep learning and lightweighting design.

📢 Student recruitment

My team “Remote Sensing Intelligence and Computing” led by Professor Heng-Chao Li admits 7–10 master’s students and 1–2 PhD students each year. We also offer research training opportunities for interested undergraduate students. Self-motivated students are welcome to contact me.
